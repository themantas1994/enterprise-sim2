# Enterprise Engineering Simulator 2

A cross-platform (Windows, Linux, Android) engineering-station simulator for three starships:
USS Enterprise NCC-1701-A, NCC-1701-D and (optionally) NCC-1701-E. Each ship has its own panels, its own
systems model and the visual language of its era.

> **Fan-made, not affiliated with Paramount/CBS.** Star Trek and all related names, ships and designs are
> trademarks of their respective owners. This is a non-commercial fan project. It uses original artwork
> and free fonts, and it contains no text or images from the books or shows.

## Status

Phase 1: source ledger for the 1701-D, engine decision and project skeleton. Nothing is playable yet.
